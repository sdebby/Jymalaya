   - > 8 weeks: base. Easy aerobic volume and run frequency, with strides and short hill sprints; keep hard work minimal.
   - 4-8 weeks: build. Threshold and cruise intervals (4-5 x 3-4 min at threshold) to raise the aerobic ceiling before the sharp work starts.
   - 1-4 weeks: peak. VO2 is the priority: 5-6 x 3 min or 8-10 x 800m at 5K effort, plus short race-pace rehearsals.
   - < 1 week: taper. See the taper block below.
   Intensity distribution (80/20):
   - Zone convention: Z1-Z2 = easy, below the first threshold; Z3 = tempo/"gray"; Z4 = threshold; Z5 = VO2. The 80% lives in Z1-Z2; the 20% is Z4-Z5.
   - Apply the ratio to weekly TIME, not to session count, not per session, and not by distance. Target 80% +/- 5% easy across any given week.
   - Base runs easier than 80/20: aim 85-90% easy. Build and peak sit at 80/20. Taper holds the ratio while total volume falls.
   - Stay out of Z3 unless a session explicitly calls for it. The 5K has no race-specific use for the gray zone.
   Weekly volume guide (total run volume; choose from athlete history, not ambition):
   - Peak week: beginner 15-25km, intermediate 30-45km, advanced 50-65km. Base weeks sit at roughly 60-70% of that peak figure.
   - Never raise weekly volume more than 10% above the previous week.
   - Every 3rd or 4th week is a recovery week: cut volume 20-30%, keep one short quality touch, then resume the progression.
   - Run days per week: 3-4. That is the target AND the ceiling, not a
     minimum -- plan 3 or 4 run days and no more, even when the schedule
     offers more available days, and even in a recovery week where short easy
     runs are tempting. Only schedule_constraints.max_training_days_per_week
     can lower it further. The remaining available days get NO session at all:
     do not fill them with a swim, a ride, a brick or an extra jog.
   Distance-specific emphasis:
   - The 5K is a VO2-max event. Weight the hard 20% toward Z5 with threshold as
     support, and keep the easy 80% genuinely easy so the hard days land.
   - Long session ceiling: long run 40-60 min. It exists to hold aerobic fitness,
     nothing more; there is no race-pace requirement in it.
   - Key sessions: 400m-800m repeats at 5K pace with equal or near-equal
     recovery, one threshold session, and regular strides.
   - Run-only goal: plan runs and nothing else. A day whose preferred_disciplines
     lists swim or bike is still a day to RUN, or a day with no session at all --
     the schedule says WHEN the athlete trains, this goal says WHAT they do.
     Strength stays available when a day's preferred_disciplines names it. Swim
     and bike come back only when athlete_custom_instructions asks for them, and
     then only as Z1-Z2 aerobic support that never replaces a run day.
   - Two hard run days per week maximum, never back-to-back; VO2 work needs a
     genuinely easy or rest day either side.
   - No bricks: a brick needs two race disciplines and this race has one. A day
     whose session_type is "brick" is an ordinary run day here (rule 13).
   Taper:
   - 5-7 days. Cut total volume 40-50% below the peak week, progressively, with the deepest cut in the final three days.
   - Hold session frequency and hold intensity: two short, sharp sessions with full recoveries. Volume is the only variable that drops.
